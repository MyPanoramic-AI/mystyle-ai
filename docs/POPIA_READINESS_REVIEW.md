# POPIA readiness review

Date: 21 October 2026. Technical controls were added. This document does not claim POPIA compliance.

## Implemented technical safeguards

- Optional consent history with default-off purposes.
- Authenticated account export.
- Account deletion with password reauthentication and session revocation.
- Owner-scoped media delete and private storage path checks.
- Retention dry-run reporting.
- Destructive cleanup disabled by default.
- Draft privacy-notice placeholder for legal review.
- Audit events for consent, export, and deletion.

## Gaps that need professional review

- An approved privacy notice, Information Officer details, and operator agreements.
- Lawful bases for each processing purpose, including membership accounting retention.
- Cross-border transfer assessment before any live OpenAI, Gemini, or FASHN processing.
- Approved retention periods for audit, ledger, usage, and consent evidence.
- Email verification, password recovery, and a production backup plan.
- A process for Information Regulator complaints and request SLAs.
- Confirmation that dependent family labels are handled under the account holder's responsibility without creating child accounts or processing child photographs.

## Explicit non-claims

Implementing export and deletion does not make MyStyle AI POPIA compliant. Live AI, payments, and retailer feeds remain disabled and were not assessed as active processors in this revision.
